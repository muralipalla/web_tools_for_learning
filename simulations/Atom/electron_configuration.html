Electronic Configuration Click Game

Click the side-by-side ↑ and ↓ slots inside each orbital box. This version covers atomic numbers 1 to 118. Higher shells are shown higher on the page: \(1s\) is at the bottom, \(2s,2p\) above it, \(3s,3p,3d\) above that, then \(4s,4p,4d,4f\), and so on.
