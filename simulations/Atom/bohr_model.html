Hydrogen Spectral Lines: Random Photon Excitation

Photons arrive from random directions. One random collision excites the electron to a higher Bohr orbit. The electron rotates about 1–2 times in that excited orbit and then falls to a lower orbit, emitting one horizontal photon. The photon passes through the grating and forms a spectral line. Orbit radii use the proportions r₁=r₀, r₂=1.5r₀, r₃=2.5r₀, r₄=4r₀, r₅=6r₀, r₆=8.5r₀.
