J. J. Thomson Cathode-Ray Experiment

A cathode-ray beam passes through electric and magnetic fields. The simulation integrates the electron path using the Lorentz force, \(F=q(E+v\times B)\), and shows how Thomson could balance the deflections and estimate the electron charge-to-mass ratio \(e/m\).

How the measurement works

Balance condition: \(eE=evB\Rightarrow v=E/B\). Magnetic curvature: \(evB=mv^2/r\Rightarrow e/m=v/(Br)\).

The button Set B to balance E chooses the magnetic field that nearly cancels electric deflection. In measurement view, the simulation fits a circular arc in the magnetic-field region and estimates \(e/m\).
