Retarded Electric Field of an Oscillating Charge

The charge starts near the left-side origin at t = 0 and then executes a small sinusoidal oscillation along the vertical y-axis. At every animation frame, the electric field vectors are computed on a grid from the retarded charge position, velocity, and acceleration using the Liénard–Wiechert electric field formula. This is a 2D slice through the 3D field. To avoid a misleading extra overlay, separate decorative wavefront circles are not shown.

What is being calculated?

E = q [ (n − β)(1 − β²)/(κ³R²) + n × ((n − β) × β̇)/(cκ³R) ] evaluated at the retarded time.

Here R points from the retarded charge position to the observation point, n = R/R, β = v/c, κ = 1 − n·β. The first term is the velocity/near field and behaves roughly as 1/R². The second term is the acceleration/radiation field and behaves as 1/R. The simulation uses normalized visual units, so it is intended for conceptual and qualitative teaching rather than numerical metrology. Separate wavefront circles have been removed because they do not directly represent contours of the full Liénard–Wiechert electric field.
