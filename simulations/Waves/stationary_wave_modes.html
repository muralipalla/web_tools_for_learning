Physics idea

Right-moving: y = A sin(kx − ωt + φ),   Left-moving: y = A sin(kx + ωt + φ)

For a fixed string, the allowed 1D modes are yₙ(x,t)=A sin(nπx/L) cos(ωₙt). The mode number n gives n antinodes and n+1 nodes including the fixed ends.
